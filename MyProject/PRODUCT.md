# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Customers** — browsing food, building a cart, checking out, tracking orders. Usually hungry, on mobile, wanting a fast and frictionless experience that feels personal and appetizing.

**Admin staff** — managing products, orders, users, and delivery options. In a back-office context, needing clarity and efficiency rather than decoration.

## Product Purpose

A food ordering platform for a home-kitchen business built on family recipes. The app exists to translate the warmth, care, and personal touch of homemade food into a digital experience — making it easy for customers to discover dishes, place orders, and track delivery, while giving the kitchen a simple admin dashboard to run daily operations.

## Brand Personality

Warm, appetizing, human. Not sterile or corporate — this is a family kitchen that happens to have an app. The tone is friendly and generous, like someone inviting you to try their cooking.

## Anti-references

- **Cold & corporate** — sterile whites, blues, gray-scale minimalism. No hospital-like or bank-like feel. Avoid the sleek-but-cold SaaS aesthetic.
- **Generic fast-food** — no cartoonish burger-joint clichés, no cheap clip-art food icons. Should feel distinctive and handcrafted, not templated.
- **Overdesigned** — no glassmorphism, gradients-over-photos, or decorative embellishments that get in the way of ordering.

## Design Principles

1. **Taste you can see** — colors, typography, and imagery should evoke appetite and warmth. The brand red (#d82405) should feel like a rich tomato or paprika, not an alarm.
2. **Family kitchen, not factory** — the design should feel personal and handcrafted, not automated or templated. Subtle handmade touches (rounded corners, warm shadows, organic spacing) over rigid grid-perfect minimalism.
3. **Speed respects hunger** — ordering flow should be frictionless. Customers are hungry; every extra tap or wait is a tax on that patience. Prioritize performance and reduce cognitive load.
4. **Mobile-first, kitchen-second** — most customers order from a phone; most admin happens on desktop. Optimize the customer path for mobile, the admin path for clarity.
5. **Trust through transparency** — order tracking, pricing, delivery estimates should feel open and honest. No hidden fees, no confusing states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum
- Support for RTL layout (Arabic-first audience)
- Touch-friendly targets on mobile (min 44px)
- Clear contrast for body text against backgrounds
